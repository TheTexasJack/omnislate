# The Last Class: Briefs

Player-facing text only.  Pulled from Operation Halas Castigation: The Last Class.
Each brief starts with `## brief-id`.  Fields come first, then `###` sections.
Sections accept plain lines (paragraphs) and `- ` lines (list items).

## tlc-class-3

- **Title:** Class 3: Mechanical Engineering
- **Type:** Training assignment
- **Location:** Mech Bays
- **Issued By:** Halas Academy Faculty
- **World:** halas

### Objective

Build and verify a legal LL0 training frame.  Every cadet must be able to point to Speed, Evasion, E-Defense, Sensors, Heat Cap, Structure, Stress, Repairs, mounts, SP, and Core Power without searching the entire page.

### Build Checklist

- Choose Everest, Sagarmatha, or Chomolungma.
- Apply HASE bonuses to the frame's statistics.
- Confirm the total number and type of mounts.
- Install legal GMS weapons without exceeding mount limits.
- Install GMS systems without exceeding SP.
- Record talents that modify weapons, movement, reactions, or tech actions.
- Record Repair Cap, Heat Cap, Save Target, Structure, Stress, and CP.
- Read every weapon and system tag aloud once.
- Explain what the pilot expects to do on a normal turn.

### Instructor Checkout

- What target number is used to shoot this mech?
- What target number is used to hack it?
- How far can it move before actions?
- Which weapon can it Skirmish with?
- What does it do when heat or HP becomes dangerous?

### Notes

The academy expects cadets to revise a training loadout after testing it.

## tlc-class-4

- **Title:** Class 4: Combat Simulation
- **Type:** Training exercise
- **Location:** Simsphere
- **Issued By:** Instructor Kael Vasik, Combat Simulation Instructor
- **World:** halas

### Objective

End Round 3 with at least one pilot in the central objective zone.  Destroying drones is useful but not the primary objective.

### Simulation Parameters

- Map: 20 by 20 spaces with marked hard cover, two soft-cover zones, and one strip of difficult terrain.
- Deployment: south edge.
- Duration: three full rounds.
- Drones respawn at the east edge if the field is cleared early.

### Known Hostiles

- Assault, Tier 1, Grunt template.  One drone per pilot, deployed in the east half.

### Simulation Safety

Real HP, heat, Structure, and Stress procedures apply inside the simulation.  When the sim ends, everything resets.  Nothing carries out of the Simsphere.

## tlc-combat-2

- **Title:** Betrayal Unseen
- **Type:** Sitrep: Holdout
- **Location:** Academic Wing landing zone
- **Issued By:** Group Commander
- **World:** halas

### Objective

Hold the landing zone until the Keshar emergency shuttle lands.

### Victory Conditions

- The shuttle lands after round 6.
- Score starts at 4.
- Every enemy inside the marked zone at the end removes 1 point.
- Win at 1 or more.  Lose below 1.

### Intelligence

- Hostile reserves may arrive at the start of any round.
- Reserve composition and arrival edge are unknown.
