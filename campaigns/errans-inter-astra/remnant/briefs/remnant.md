## remnant-orders

- **Title:** Recovery Order: Magnus Contact
- **Type:** Mission brief
- **Location:** Magnus, high orbit
- **World:** magnus

### Situation

- A recovered OmniNet drift packet reached the Lance through an outer-system relay buoy.  Its authentication is corrupted and most of it is still encrypted.
- Magnus traffic control logged a cold, dark capital hull holding about 384,000 km off the planet.  Registry lists it as lost.
- The contact does not answer hails.

### Goal

Your orders: trace the packet to its source, board the vessel, assess it, and secure it.
What the Lance hopes to get out of this is yours to decide.

### Stakes

- Will the Lance learn who sent the packet, and why it found you?
- Will you secure the hull before someone else strips it bare?
- What happened to the crew of a ship listed as lost?
- Will you make it out before the ghosts consume your soul?

### Reserves

- A sealed Emergency Recovery and Disposition Warrant.

### Boots on the Ground

- Approach the contact and dock.
- Board at mech scale.  Expect damaged systems, failed doors, and vacuum.
- Reach engineering and command, and find out what is still running.
