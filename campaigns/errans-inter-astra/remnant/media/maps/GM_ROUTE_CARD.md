# GM route card / REMNANT v1.3

**IMPORTANT:** Navigation diagrams show network connectivity, not physical hull position or a numbered-deck blueprint. See the guide for outcome-dependent decisions and access contingencies.

### Main exploration / flexible order

1. **Board:** `P-01` damaged service hangar / mech maintenance lock.
2. **Traverse:** `P-02` central transit network, showing conflicting AR routes, gantries, tram links and true physical landmarks.
3. **Arm Lock 1:** `P-02 → D-01` Deimos docking spine; access the air-gapped isolation interlock before the engineering operation. If missed, it remains physically accessible later at additional cost.
4. **Optional salvage conflict:** `P-02 → P-03` cargo-transfer gallery, occupied by independent pirates. Six-round salvage interception or another resolution. Losing salvage costs parts/clues rather than trapping progression.
5. **Secure Lock 2:** `P-02 → P-04` Proxima central engineering transit bay and eastern hardwired control enclosure. Hell's Kraven may be negotiated with or defeated through the eight-round GAUNTLET. This area remains reachable through other plausible plans if a fight is lost.
6. **Repair and preparation:** `P-04 / P-02 → P-05` fabrication / ship printer. This beat lets the Lancers prepare for one long final fight. The optional MED BAY and crew support branch can hold scene images and crew phase-echo sightings.
7. **Separate caskets:** Lock 3 involves REM's casket aboard Proxima and RHANT's aboard Deimos. Local visits via `P-06` and `D-04` are conditional, not mandatory sightseeing; the engineered hardwired sequence can cycle both if intact.
8. **Finale:** return along the docking route `P-02 → D-01 → D-02 → D-03`. When the prototype Lance goes offline at Lock 3, the hitchhiker manifests in Deimos's Lance bay. The Lancers face *The Seam*, not REMNANT as an automatic boss.

### GM fallback routes and optional branches

- `P-01 → pilot-only access`: provide dismounted alternatives, not mandatory mech abandonment.
- `P-02 → P-03 → P-02`: cargo detour can be negotiated or skipped.
- `P-04 → P-06`: REM cabinet only if local manual operation is needed.
- `D-02 → D-04`: RHANT cabinet only if local manual operation is needed.
- `P-02 / P-05 → MED BAY`: atmosphere, crew clues, or exploration only; the guide does not fix its exact deck location.
- `D-03 → emergency exit`: an optional route to preserve the guide's explicit retreat outcome in the final fight.

### GM truth / do not show prematurely

The scientist wrote the warning, PROMETHEUS delivered it, and the personalized GRAVIS phenomenon remains unexplained. REMNANT is an unexpected third unshackled person, not a horror creature that deliberately massacred the crew. The hitchhiker is the final monster, and the majority of missing crew are out of phase rather than definitively dead. The prototype is on **Deimos**. REM and RHANT remain separate people and lose memory of the fusion after successful cycling.

### Visual consistency checklist

- Treat all drawings as **NTS / not to scale**.
- Every `P-xx` scan is part of Proxima; every `D-xx` scan is part of Deimos.
- Labels are navigation IDs, **not literal contiguous deck numbers**.
- Keep broad passages mech-capable and limit small crawlways to pilots.
- Large blank structural volumes are intentional. They represent hull infrastructure, voids, and inaccessible sections, not “missing rooms” to be packed with furniture.
- Never use a 40k-cathedral-size ship as a small battlemat trapped inside a silhouette.
