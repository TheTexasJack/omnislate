# **Operation Halas Castigation**
Halas Academy was a faction-neutral military school on the remote diasporan world of Halas.  Joined under the command of Gen, Gellimex Demingsworth, its first candidate class includes cadets from Union, diasporan communities, the Karrakin Trade Baronies, Harrison Armory, cosmopolitan fleets, and independent stations.  The academy’s founding idea is simple: train excellent pilots together, then let shared experience become stronger than inherited faction loyalty.

**-={ Notable NPCS }=-**
**Pyretica - Commander Veles Feuergeber** [FOY-er-GAY-buh]
Has bilateral congenital lower-limb amelia, meaning born without legs. They use sophisticated mechanical prosthetic legs and is otherwise fully capable, including as a mech pilot. Their condition is complicated by an extremely rare disorder formally called: Somatic Morphogenic Exclusion Syndrome (SMES).
SMES prevents biological reconstruction of his missing limbs. Attempts to print or regenerate organic legs fail in abnormal ways. Tissue may initially form, but neural organization, vascular development, and morphogenic signaling collapse. Advanced medical printers may refuse the procedure entirely, identifying the requested anatomy as incompatible with the patient's biological template. Mechanical prosthetics work normally because the rejection appears limited to organic morphogenesis.
During the attack, her commander, Haruk Severin of Delta Squad, died in a building collapse.  Already ahead of other cadets, Veles was promoted to Commander.
Pyretica runs a Gengis variant and focuses heavily on Flames and NHPs.  As an Iconoclast, Technophile, her two NHPs: Dracarys, her unstable Iconoclast NHP with a flame themed memetic spark; Magnus, Her Student Class Technophile NHP.
