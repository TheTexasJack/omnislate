World art baked into the session file by the build.  Named <slug>-<slot>.png (or .jpg, .webp).

  kestrel-map.png          2:1 equirectangular map for the globe.  Keep the top and bottom edges plain ice or ocean.
  kestrel-thermal.png      still for the thermal panel and Thermal mode
  kestrel-surface.png      still for the main drone feed
  kestrel-description.png, -natural, -settlement, -anthropocentric, -observed, -planetside, -life   optional panel art
  kestrel-planet.png       optional fallback when a world has no map

Images and video are artwork only.  Keep all text and labels out of them.
