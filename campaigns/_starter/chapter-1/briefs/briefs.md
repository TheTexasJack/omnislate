# Briefs

Player-facing text only.  Anything written here can end up on the table screen.
Each brief starts with `## brief-id` (lowercase, numbers, dashes).  Fields come first, then `###` sections.
Sections take plain lines (paragraphs) and `- ` lines (list items).
`World` is optional.  When it names a world in the session, the brief shows that world's gravity, atmosphere, and temperature.

## example-orders

- **Title:** Survey Kestrel Station
- **Type:** Mission brief
- **Location:** Kestrel Station
- **Issued By:** Survey Command
- **World:** kestrel

### Objective

Escort the survey team to Turbine Ridge and confirm why the station's power output dropped by a third overnight.

### Known Details

- The station reports no hostile contact.
- Two turbines stopped turning at 0300 local time.
- Wind on the shelf gusts past ninety kilometers an hour.

## example-intel

- **Title:** Decrypted: Ridge Maintenance Log
- **Type:** Intercepted data
- **Issued By:** Recovered packet

### Contents

The maintenance crew was ordered off the ridge two days before the shutdown.  The order carries no signature.

## example-emergency

- **Title:** Emergency: Station Under Attack
- **Type:** Emergency order
- **Location:** Kestrel Station
- **Issued By:** Station Security
- **World:** kestrel

### Orders

Unknown mechs are moving up the canyon toward the landing field.  Hold the rim until the evacuation shuttle lifts.

### Priorities

- Protect the shuttle pad.
- Keep the canyon road clear for civilians.
- Report enemy numbers when you have them.
