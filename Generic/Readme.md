## R.O.C.K. idler notes

- One self-contained file.  Open it in any browser, phone or desktop.
- Tap R.O.C.K. to poke him.  Drag him to carry and throw him.  Tap or swipe the debris to stir it up or undo his work.
- Keys: Space smashes now, N starts a new shape, F is full screen, M is sound, H is help.
- Add your own short words for him to build: `rock-idler.html?words=GG,HI` or edit `CUSTOM_WORDS` at the top of the script.
- Sound starts off.  The screen stays awake while the page is open over https, where the browser allows it.
