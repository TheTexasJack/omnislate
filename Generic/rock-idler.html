R.O.C.K. IDLER v1.0

R.O.C.K. builds a shape out of debris, admires it, then smashes it. Then he does it again. That is the whole job.

  • Tap R.O.C.K. to poke him.
  • Drag R.O.C.K. to carry him. Let go to throw him.
  • Tap or swipe the debris to stir it up. Yes, you can undo his work.
  • Keyboard: Space smashes now. N starts a new shape. F is full screen. M is sound. H is this panel.
  • Add your own short words to the address: rock-idler.html?words=GG,HI
  • The screen stays awake while this page is open, where the browser allows it.
